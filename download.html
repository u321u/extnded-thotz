Download for macOS
First launch on Mac — one-time pass

1. Unzip and drag ExtndedThotz.app to Applications.
2. Double-click it once — macOS will warn.
3. Click Done (don't move to Trash).
4. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to the Security section, and click "Open Anyway" next to ExtndedThotz.
5. Click Open. macOS remembers this — every launch after is normal.

On first run, the app may download the Whisper base model once (about 145 MB) so dictation works offline from then on. Windows SmartScreen may show an "unknown publisher" warning until the build is code-signed — this is expected for unsigned executables and safe to click through after verifying the checksum. On macOS, the equivalent warning is bypassed once via Privacy & Security → "Open Anyway".

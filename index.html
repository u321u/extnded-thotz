Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Private by default

Transcription runs on your machine with a local Whisper model. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is logged on a server.

Clean, punctuated text

Filler words are trimmed and punctuation is applied automatically, so what lands at your cursor reads like you typed it.

Global hotkey

Ctrl+Windows out of the box — or record your own. Hold-to-talk or toggle mode, whichever fits your workflow.

Voice-reactive overlay

A subtle overlay reacts to your voice while it listens, so you always know exactly when you're being transcribed.

Clipboard intact

Your clipboard is saved before the paste and restored after. Copy something, dictate, and it's still there.

Portable & instant

A single ~22 MB executable. No installer, no Python, no wizard. Download, run, start dictating in seconds.

Questions?

Quick answers

Does it send audio to the cloud?

No. Transcription runs entirely on your machine with local Whisper. Your audio never leaves your computer.

Do I need Python installed?

No. It's a portable Windows executable — download, run, done.

Does it work in all Windows apps?

Yes, virtually any app that accepts text input. If an app runs as Administrator, run Extnded Thotz as Administrator too (Windows UIPI restriction).
